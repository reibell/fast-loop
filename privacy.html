Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

Fast Loop (“the App”) is a fasting timer and wellness habit tracker. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

1. Information stored on your device

Fast Loop is designed to work primarily on your device. Fasting sessions, timer settings, plans, notes, and related app preferences are stored locally on your device. The current version does not operate a Fast Loop account server or send this local tracking information to a Fast Loop backend.

If you choose to create an optional in-app account, the account details are stored locally on that device. Passwords are not stored in plain text; the App stores a cryptographic hash for local sign-in. This local account is not an online account and does not provide cross-device synchronization.

2. Notifications

If you allow notifications, Fast Loop may schedule local notifications on your device for timer reminders. Notification permission can be managed in your device settings.

3. Subscriptions and payments

Fast Loop Pro subscriptions are offered through Apple’s in-app purchase system. Payment processing and subscription management are handled by Apple under its terms and privacy practices. Fast Loop does not receive or store your full payment card details. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple account settings.

4. Data sharing and advertising

The current version does not include third-party advertising or a Fast Loop-operated analytics or advertising profile. Fast Loop does not sell your personal information. Information you choose to send to us by email will be used to respond to your request.

5. Data retention and deletion

App data is retained locally on your device until you remove it within the App, where that option is available, or remove the App and its associated data through your device. Device backup settings may affect whether local data is included in a device backup. We cannot remotely access or delete data stored only on your device.

6. Children’s privacy

Fast Loop is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through a Fast Loop-operated online service.

7. Security

We use the local-storage and security measures implemented in the App. No method of electronic storage is completely secure. Please keep your device and operating system up to date.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the App changes. The effective date above will be revised when a new version is published.
