Privacy Policy

Fast Loop · Effective date: November 13, 2026

1. About this policy

Fast Loop (the “App”) is a fasting timer and wellness habit-tracking app. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

2. Information you enter and store

Depending on the features you use, you may enter or create fasting plans and history, fasting start and end times, durations, weight entries, progress photos, water-intake records, notes, preferences, reminders, and app settings.

The App is designed to store these records locally on your device. The developer does not operate an account server or cloud service to sync these records. Your entries and photos are not uploaded to servers operated by the developer.

3. Notifications

If you allow notifications, Fast Loop may schedule local notifications on your device, such as fasting reminders. You can manage notification permissions in your device settings and reminders in the App.

4. Purchases and subscriptions

If you purchase a subscription, the transaction is processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple may process information under its own privacy policy and terms. Fast Loop does not receive your full payment-card details.

Apple’s StoreKit services may provide the App with subscription status and transaction information so the App can provide features associated with your purchase. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account subscription settings.

5. Sharing and exporting

The App may let you export records, such as a CSV file, or share an image or card using features provided by your device. These actions are initiated by you. Once you choose to save, share, or send information outside the App, the destination app or service handles it under its own privacy policy.

6. Information collected by the developer

The developer does not operate a backend service for collecting or storing your fasting records, weight entries, progress photos, water records, or notes. This policy does not cover information that Apple or other platform providers process independently, such as App Store transactions or operating-system diagnostics, or services you choose when sharing or exporting content.

Fast Loop is not designed to sell your personal information. The developer does not sell the information you enter into the App.

7. Retention and deletion

Information stored locally in the App generally remains on your device until you delete it within the App, where that option is available, or remove the App and its locally stored data. Export information you wish to keep before deleting the App or changing devices, if an export feature is available.

The developer cannot remotely retrieve or delete records that remain only on your device.

8. Children’s privacy

Fast Loop is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the developer, contact us using the details below.

9. Security

Fast Loop is designed to keep the records described above on your device. You are responsible for securing your device, including its passcode and access controls. No method of electronic storage is guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the App changes or legal requirements evolve. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
